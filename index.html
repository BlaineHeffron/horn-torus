How the planes work

Each angle θk is a rotation in one plane. θ1 rotates the fixed d1–d2 plane, around the ring. θk for k ≥ 2 rotates the plane spanned by rk−1, the outward direction of the level above, and the new axis dk+1. That is the trip around level k's tube. The plane moves with the levels above it, so the frame is nested, like gimbals.

Turns nk is how many full rotations that plane makes per trip around the loop. It can be a fraction such as 3/2 (denominators up to 12). Then the loop goes around 2 times before it closes: once around per denominator. Drift δk is how fast that rotation slides in time, in cycles per beat. The whole loop is drawn at once, so drift is what you see move.

The head is a point that travels the loop once every C beats (the fractal cycle). Its rotation speed in plane k is ωk = nk / C + δk cycles per beat. That's the "head speed" column. To get a speed ω you want: pick C, set nk = round(ω·C), then set δk = ω − nk/C.

Picture rotations are a separate, rigid rotation of the whole 9D space in any fixed coordinate plane di–dj. The camera then shows d1, d2, d3 in perspective, and color shows the hidden axes.

4D fractal

A quaternion Julia set q → q² + c, ray-marched through the same picture rotations and camera. Its constant c comes from the head point. The head's angle around the ring walks c along the edge of the Mandelbrot set's main cardioid, where Julia sets have the most detail. Its distance from the axis sets how close c sits to that edge, and its d3 and d4 tilt c's imaginary part into the quaternion j and k axes. So the fractal cycles when the loop does. The head's d5 slides the 3D slice through the fourth axis. Color is the fractal's fourth coordinate, the same way the loop's hidden axes are colored.

Formula

Kleinian: the horn torus inverted through its pinch is a cylinder. The fractal is that cylinder's orbit under a translation along its axis (tube turns set the period), a k-fold rotation (k = ring turns), a second translation for 3+ angles, and one inversion sphere that the head steers, all inverted back. Every piece is a Möbius image of the horn torus, and they pile up at the pinch. Edge distance sets the sphere's size.

Point fractals are drawn like the curve: same 9D space, picture rotations, camera and hidden-axis colors, and they run through the trails and kaleidoscope. Horn-torus fractal: each nesting level of the torus is the map p → ½(±shift + rotatek(±θk) p) in plane d1–dk+2. Applying those maps in every order gives a horn torus of horn tori, with the head's current angles θk, so it moves with the loop. Julia: points from running z → ±√(z − c) backward in the scene's dimension, with c from the head. Fractal mix is brightness, zoom is size, and Solidity, d5 slice and Focus don't apply.

Oscillators (beat-synced)

Each oscillator sweeps one slider around its set value, in time with the beat. Depth is a share of that slider's full range. Oscillators are saved with the show.

Sound → parameters

Works while Listen to room or Analyze a track is on. The sound is split into six frequency bands plus Volume (all frequencies, fast) and Loudness (all frequencies, slow: quiet vs loud parts of a song). Each is auto-leveled so it reads 0 (quiet) to 1 (loud). Loudness compares against about the last minute, so it suits building complexity: link it to trace length, focus depth, θ speeds, kaleidoscope or IFS copies. A link pushes a slider up by depth × that band's level; a negative depth pushes it down. Links stack with oscillators and are saved with the show.
